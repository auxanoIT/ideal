# Data Centre Hero Image

## Generation Prompt

Create a realistic editorial photograph for a Nigerian data centre infrastructure company. Show an active enterprise data centre aisle with organised server racks, visible fibre and copper patching, clean cable management, and subtle operational activity. A Nigerian Ideal Solutions technical engineer should be working at rack level in the foreground, inspecting or connecting equipment with a professional posture. Other server racks should remain visibly powered and operational in the background. The scene should communicate real technical work happening inside a live data centre, not a showroom or empty server room.

Use a premium but natural photographic style, realistic lighting, neutral graphite and cool steel tones with restrained warm practical lighting, accurate equipment proportions, and enough negative space on the left side for website hero copy. Keep the engineer and rack work on the right half of the frame. Use a wide 16:9 composition suitable for a full-width website hero, with sharp detail around the active rack and a naturally softer background.

## Avoid

- Generic server-room renders or artificial sci-fi lighting
- Empty racks, disconnected cables, or visibly abandoned equipment
- Stock-photo posing or exaggerated facial expressions
- Data centre exteriors, telecom towers, traders, or unrelated office scenes
- Logos, watermarks, readable fake labels, or invented certifications
- Excessive blue glow, neon effects, haze, or cinematic darkness
