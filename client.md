# Ideal Solutions client checklist

- Confirm content approvers and platform owners; use collaborator invitations.
- Verify canonical domain: https://www.idealsolutions.com.ng.
- Confirm email info@idealsolutions.com.ng and approved phone/WhatsApp details.
- Use the single address: 21, Abeokuta Street, Off Obasa Street,
  Oba Akran Avenue, Ikeja, Lagos.
- Configure project values from `.env.example`; keep secrets private.
- Verify Sanity access, publishing and webhooks using [the guide](docs/sanity-setup.md).
- Verify Ideal Solutions forms, notification inbox, email sender and footer in HubSpot.
- Obtain approval and rights for portraits, client logos, project results and media.
- Approve legal copy and consent behaviour.
- Check production enquiries, email receipt, mobile navigation and
  [SEO launch requirements](docs/seo-launch-checklist.md).
